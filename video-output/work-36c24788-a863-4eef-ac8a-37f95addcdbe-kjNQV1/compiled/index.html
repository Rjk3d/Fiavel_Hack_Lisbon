Finding a home should be exciting.
But for tenants and landlords,
a rental deposit can create uncertainty,
mistrust,
and disputes.
Fiável starts with a simple question:
what if the rules were clear
from day one?
Fiável is building
a more transparent way to rent.
It brings lease conditions,
payments,
deposit protection,
and guided actions into one connected experience.
Both sides can follow the rental journey:
what was agreed,
what has been paid,
what is due,
and what happens when a problem arises.
Less ambiguity.
More accountability.
Underneath Fiável is LeaseVault,
a Soroban smart contract on Stellar Testnet.
It is designed to hold the rental guarantee
and enforce defined rules for payments,
claims,
deadlines,
and releases.
When an action happens on-chain,
its transaction can be independently verified.
Fiável's agents help tenants
and landlords understand their lease,
review deadlines,
and prepare their next steps.
They explain the available rules;
they do not replace the contract
or take control away from the people involved.
Where supported,
Fiável explores putting eligible deposit funds
to work through Blend,
with explicit consent and clear limits.
Investment involves risk,
and returns are never guaranteed.
Trust grows through accountable behavior.
Fiável is also exploring rental reputation,
so both sides can make
better-informed decisions using transparent,
appropriately verified history.
By combining rental-focused smart contracts,
Stellar payments,
guided agents,
and a transparent user experience,
Fiável brings blockchain technology to
a problem people actually understand.
Fiável.
Fair rentals,
built on trust.
Built on Stellar.
